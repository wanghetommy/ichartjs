# Repository Development Instructions

Before changing this repository, read and follow [the seven development principles](docs/agent/development/development-principles.md).

Use [the Development Guide](docs/agent/development-guide.md) and the relevant scenario/iteration document to determine the change scope and acceptance checks. The principles are maintained in one place; do not copy them into separate rule sets.

These instructions apply to development of iChart.js itself, not to applications consuming the package or ordinary use of its Skill.
